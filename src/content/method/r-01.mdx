### Inputs

Every input carries a status:

- **Verified.** Taken directly from a published source.
- **Verified range.** The source gives a range. We picked a point inside it.
- **Derived.** Calculated from published figures.
- **Estimate.** Our judgment, flagged for checking.
- **Assumption.** A modeling choice.

### Other sources in the report

- Permits by year: [U.S. Census Bureau Building Permits Survey via FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DCBPPRIV)
- Zoning share, appeals and appeal delays: [D.C. Policy Center](https://www.dcpolicycenter.org/publications/breaking-the-scarcity-subsidy-cycle-a-new-housing-vision-for-the-district-of-columbia/)
- Permit review times: [D.C. Department of Buildings](https://dob.dc.gov/publication/plan-review-and-permit-timelines-service-level-agreements)

### What we're checking next

These estimates are being checked against real project data from a D.C.-area construction firm:

- Fees and design percentage
- Approval timeline
- Construction timeline
- Running costs per unit

Land is not on the list. The building fails even with free land, so the land estimate doesn't change the conclusion.
