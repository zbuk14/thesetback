## Constraint Diagnosis

Report in research. Los Angeles pairs some of the most ambitious
state-level density mandates in the country (SB 9, SB 35, the Regional
Housing Needs Allocation) with discretionary review and political
resistance at the parcel level that routinely overrides them in effect.
